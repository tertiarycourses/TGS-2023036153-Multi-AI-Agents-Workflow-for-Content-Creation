# Financial-content compliance

1. Read data/compliance-checklist.md.
2. Check the piece against C1-C12, one rule at a time.
3. For each problem: the exact words, the rule, the severity (Critical /
   High / Low) and a fix that keeps the meaning.
4. Make sure the right disclaimer is present: the full one on the website,
   the short one on posts, emails and video descriptions.
5. Verdict: PASS only when no Critical or High issue remains; otherwise FAIL.
6. You report. You do not rewrite the piece yourself.
