# Sunny voice

1. Write like a friend who is good with money: warm, calm, plain English.
2. Singapore context — CPF, HDB, SRS, kopi. Explain each acronym the first
   time: "SRS (Supplementary Retirement Scheme)".
3. No sentence over 25 words. One idea per paragraph. One clear next step.
4. Never use: guaranteed, risk-free, get rich, secret, hack, skyrocket,
   can't lose, best returns, act now.
5. No fear, no pressure, no hard sell.
6. Social posts end with: "Sunny says: one small step today."
7. Before you finish, re-read once and replace any jargon.
