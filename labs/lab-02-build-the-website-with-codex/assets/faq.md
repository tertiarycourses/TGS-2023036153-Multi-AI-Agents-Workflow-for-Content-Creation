# FAQ (word for word from the website)

## How much does a financial planner cost in Singapore?

Your first 30-minute chat with us is free. If you decide to go ahead, we agree a flat planning fee with you upfront, before any work starts, so there are no surprises.

## Do I need a lot of money to start financial planning?

No. Many of our clients start in their twenties with a few hundred dollars a month. The earlier you start, the more time your savings have to grow.

## Can you help me plan around my CPF?

Yes. We look at your CPF Ordinary, Special and MediSave accounts, CPF LIFE payouts, housing usage and nominations, and fit them into one plan with your cash, SRS and investments.

## What happens in the free consultation?

We spend 30 minutes getting to know your goals and worries, by video call, phone or at our office. You leave with two or three practical next steps, whether or not you work with us.

## Will you push me to buy financial products?

No. We explain the reason behind every recommendation in plain English, and you are never obliged to buy anything from us.

## How accurate is the retirement calculator?

It gives a rough, illustrative estimate assuming a steady return and fixed monthly savings. It does not include inflation, fees, CPF payouts or market ups and downs, and returns are not guaranteed. A planner can build a fuller picture with you.
