# Recommendation (sample — use your own from Lab 1 if you have it)

**Positioning:** Plain-English financial planning for busy Singapore
families — no jargon, no hard sell, a one-page plan you will actually follow.

**Target first**
1. *The mid-career family (35-49)* — juggling children's education, an HDB or
   condo loan and parents' needs. Most enquiries in firm-metrics come from
   this group, and they convert best after a free chat.
2. *The pre-retiree (50-62)* — CPF LIFE, retirement sums and "do I have
   enough?". High intent, highest-value plans.

**Channels to start with**
1. LinkedIn — professionals research advisers here; long-form posts work.
2. Facebook — families and pre-retirees; community groups; events.
3. The Sunny Sunday email — turns checklist downloads into booked chats.
YouTube follows once the first three run smoothly (one explainer a fortnight).

**Content themes:** Know your retirement number · CPF made simple ·
Protect the family · Pay less tax, legally.

**The website must:** put "Book a free chat" in view on every screen; offer
the free Money Check-up Checklist in exchange for an email; show the
retirement calculator with a clear illustration label; answer the six FAQs;
show the general-information disclaimer.

**Risks:** content read as financial advice or as promising returns (MAS
digital advertising guidelines); emailing people who did not opt in (PDPA);
publishing a wrong CPF or tax figure.
