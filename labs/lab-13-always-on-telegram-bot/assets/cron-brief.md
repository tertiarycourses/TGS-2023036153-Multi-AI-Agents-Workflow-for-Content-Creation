# The Monday content brief (Lab 13)

Every Monday at 8am Hermes prepares the week's content brief and sends it to
you for approval. It never publishes.

The brief: this week's items from strategy/calendar.csv; for each, the
persona, the key fact from facts-2026.md and a one-line hook; anything due
for review in the next 2 business days; anything still pending in
review/approvals.csv.

Reply APPROVE to accept, or tell it what to change.
