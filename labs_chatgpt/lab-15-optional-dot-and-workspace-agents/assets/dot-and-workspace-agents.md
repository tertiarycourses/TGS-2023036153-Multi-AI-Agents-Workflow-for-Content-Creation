# Optional demo — a Dot Team Lead and workspace agents (Lab 15)

Trainer demo; not assessed. Facts checked 8 October 2026.

## Dots (announced at OpenAI DevDay, 29 September 2026)
- A Dot is a persistent agent that keeps working after you close the chat,
  with its own cloud computer and browser.
- The first Dot is included on Pro and Business Premium; Enterprise, Edu
  and Healthcare can try the beta when an administrator enables it.
- Background research is read-only; sending messages or changing content
  needs your approval.
- **Custom Rules** permit an action, require approval, or prohibit it.
  Start with: block sending, deleting and posting; require approval for
  anything account-affecting.
- **Activity View** shows what the Dot did; you can step in.
- In **ChatGPT Space**, people, ChatGPT, Codex and Dots share pages, and
  you can @mention a Dot.

## Workspace agents (Business, Enterprise, Edu)
1. Click **Agents** in the sidebar and describe a workflow — e.g. the
   Growth Analyst's weekly report.
2. Add the approved apps it may use (Drive, Gmail).
3. Choose a trigger (e.g. weekly). Share it with the team.

The same rule applies: draft, a person approves, then publish.
